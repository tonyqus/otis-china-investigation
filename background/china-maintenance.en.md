# China Outside Shanghai: Maintenance and Rescue Cases

> Unofficial English translation of the research note updated November 14, 2026. [Chinese note](../docs/zh-CN/otis-china-fake-maintenance.md). Existing source status is preserved; underlying cases were not freshly reverified in this restructuring.

Scope: Otis-related maintenance and rescue matters outside Shanghai, organized by incident location and named entity. See [Shanghai research](shanghai-maintenance.en.md). Non-compliant maintenance and delayed rescue are not automatically falsified maintenance. Some original decisions remain outstanding.
