# Shanghai: First-Pass Inspection Rates, Comparable Periods in 2025–2026

> Unofficial English translation of the Chinese research note. [Chinese note](../docs/zh-CN/one-time-inspection-qualified-rate.md). No fresh verification of underlying figures was performed during translation.

The two entities are 上海奥的斯电梯有限公司 (Shanghai Otis) and 奥的斯机电电梯有限公司上海分公司 (Otis Electric, Shanghai Branch). Changes equal the 2026 figure minus the 2025 figure.

| Maintenance entity | Quarter | 2025 | 2026 | Change in percentage points |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Shanghai Otis | Q1 | 89.94% | 88.94% | -1.00 |
| Shanghai Otis | Q2* | 90.25% | 90.41% | +0.16 |
| Otis Electric, Shanghai Branch | Q1 | 88.35% | 74.00% | -14.35 |
| Otis Electric, Shanghai Branch | Q2* | 89.02% | 77.64% | -11.38 |

Q1 in both years uses the label 定期检验一次合格率, rendered here as “first-pass periodic inspection rate.”

*2025 Q2 uses 检验检测一次合格率 (“first-pass inspection/testing rate”), whereas 2026 Q2 uses 定期检验一次合格率. The displayed difference is an arithmetic comparison only; identical statistical coverage has not been established.

The data concern Shanghai maintenance entities, not manufacturer failure rates, new-elevator acceptance rates or comprehensive credit rankings. Shanghai Otis changed relatively little in these published figures; the Shanghai Branch of Otis Electric had lower figures than in the corresponding prior-year periods. The table does not establish causes.
