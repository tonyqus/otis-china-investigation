> Unofficial English translation and organized rendering of the Chinese note dated November 13, 2026. [Chinese note](../docs/zh-CN/otis-china-law-firms.md). [Civil-case details and sample](civil-litigation.en.md).

Scope: public mainland China proceedings and law-firm disclosures. Historical service records are not a current retainer list, complete panel or ranking. Different corporate and branch clients must not be conflated. A single representation does not establish continuing engagement. Firm/branch and lawyer names reflect the document date, not current affiliation.

“Judgment reproduction” means identified from a public copy, not individually authenticated against the court original. “Firm disclosure” is self-description, not independent performance assessment. No complete sample supports firm-wide win rates or identifies counsel for the user's project.

Chinese lawyer names are retained to avoid unverified English-name mappings.

## Outcome analysis: limited nine-case sample

See [the civil-litigation analysis](civil-litigation.en.md#nine-case-descriptive-results). “Do not calculate win rates” means do not generalize a retrieved sample into overall or firm-wide rates. Explicit-denominator descriptive ratios are reported separately.

All nine received some principal support; eight received the full principal remaining for adjudication (88.9%). Claims may have been reduced and counterclaim liability may coexist; this is not an 88.9% full-win rate.

Alphaleader Shanghai's C06/C07 principal support is 2/2, but the cases share Carrefour context, and defendants were absent despite written views. This cannot assess complex quality disputes. Jiuze's C01 supported approximately 4.5% of initial principal for several different reasons; C04 supported maintenance principal. Selecting one alone is misleading. Dacheng Chengdu's C05 did not overturn late-delivery liability but changed damages. C02/C03 rejected two monetary quality counterclaims for evidence/causation reasons; two cases do not establish a general defense rate.

These outcomes do not predict this project's prospects or establish employee-defamation expertise.
