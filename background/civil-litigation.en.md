## Sources and scope

The research uses public reproductions of judgments, not a set individually authenticated against court originals. First-instance outcomes are not treated as final unless the text says so; later proceedings may be unknown. The sample is not complete and cannot establish Otis's overall success rate.

Party assertions and court findings are separated. Contracts, technical facts and applicable rules are case-specific. Different Otis entities and branches must be identified individually. This material does not establish bribery.

## C01: Parts already included in maintenance

Hangzhou Shangcheng District People's Court; March 31, 2022; first instance, later proceedings unverified.

Otis sought RMB 417,785.96 plus interest under 23 T-business contract forms. The property company argued that some parts were already covered by the maintenance agreement and other items involved third-party payments and discount arrangements.

The court treated 12 forms as involving separate third-party payment/discount arrangements, leaving the proper rights holders to claim separately. Nine forms fell within replacement items in Appendix E and required no additional payment. One form lacked the original. One wire-rope form had an original, signatures/seals and performance evidence, supporting RMB 18,950 plus interest.

Compare maintenance appendices item by item with separately signed repair forms. The unsupported amount cannot all be labeled duplicate billing, unlawful charging or malicious litigation.

## C02: Quality defense and RMB 200,000 counterclaim

Guangzhou Intermediate People's Court, second instance; appeal dismissed and original judgment upheld.

The property company alleged inadequate cleaning/lubrication, failure to replace items in an overhaul quotation, slow repairs and discrepancies about bearing quantities. It counterclaimed RMB 200,000.

The court found that unilateral photographs did not establish a year-long failure to clean/lubricate, without corroborating demands for performance. Whole-system replacement in an overhaul quotation was not equivalent to routine replacement of individual wear parts. One WeChat record about an 11-day fault did not prove continuing breach; causation of the claimed loss was not established.

RMB 158,400 maintenance principal was supported. Contractual damages followed court-determined segmented rates, not Otis's original 0.5% daily claim in full. Evidence must identify equipment, periods, obligations and corresponding losses. This decision does not mean every fault or delay is minor.

## C03: Repair prevented appraisal; causation unproven

Shenyang Heping District People's Court; May 9, 2022; first instance, subsequent proceedings unverified.

The property company alleged poor maintenance and counterclaimed RMB 301,578.6 in repair costs. Otis produced maintenance forms signed by both sides' staff. An appraisal of causation was requested but returned because repairs had changed the site.

The court supported maintenance RMB 67,064.5, repairs RMB 53,866 and contractual damages RMB 21,000. Additional interest was not stacked because damages already compensated the interest loss. The counterclaim failed for lack of direct causation evidence.

Preserve pre-repair conditions, removed parts, work orders and inspection records while ensuring safety and timely repairs. Necessary safety work must not be delayed for evidence collection.

## C04: Another project's fine was not an automatic setoff

Guangzhou Intermediate People's Court; final judgment September 27, 2023.

The property company sought to offset a RMB 50,000 administrative fine from another project against RMB 20,640 maintenance fees. The projects, penalized entity and contractual parties differed; Otis did not agree. Another proceeding had involved a partial setoff, and other losses could be claimed separately. Payment of RMB 20,640 plus interest was upheld.

A problem on another project does not automatically permit setoff here. The result does not deny that the other project had problems.

## C05: Late delivery established; appeal changed damages

Chengdu Intermediate People's Court; final judgment September 2, 2021. Otis counsel: Dacheng (Chengdu), 李成军 and 李芯愉.

The delivery trigger involved signing, advance payment, ordering and payment of 75% of the price. Applying the express requirement of delivery within 20 days after payment, the court set October 7, 2019 as the last delivery day. Actual deliveries occurred October 15 and 17.

The RMB 203,000 equipment balance plus financing interest remained payable. Otis's delay compensation used the RMB 1,015,000 total price at four times contemporaneous one-year LPR, October 8–17, 2019. A contractual daily 1% of total price was excessive. The appeal corrected the first instance's extension of delay compensation until damages were paid, using actual delivery as the endpoint.

Counsel did not overturn liability, but the appeal changed the calculation. This was equipment delivery, not installation delay; four times LPR is not a universal rule.

## Remaining work and sample-expansion standard

Verify court originals, full parties, cause, dates, appeal/retrial/enforcement status and incomplete C06–C08 reasoning. The later C06/C07 checks above supplement earlier gaps. Continue searching for direct owners'-committee defendants; none confirmed is not proof none exist.

For every added dispute record project, court/date, procedural status, counsel, attendance and substantive defense, initial/final claim, supported amount, counterclaim/result, procedural ending, actual recovery and source grade. Group by dispute and defense type; include all eligible positive and negative outcomes rather than selecting wins.
