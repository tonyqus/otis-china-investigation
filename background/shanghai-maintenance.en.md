# Shanghai: Maintenance Records, Compliance and Rescue Cases

> Unofficial English translation of the Chinese research note updated November 14, 2026. [Chinese note](../docs/zh-CN/otis-shanghai-fake-maintenance.md). This translation carries forward the note's verification status; it is not an independent re-investigation.

Scope: Otis-related maintenance and rescue matters occurring in Shanghai, classified by incident location and legal entity. Non-compliant maintenance, delayed rescue and falsely completed rescue work orders are not interchangeable with proven falsified maintenance.

## Principal finding recorded in the research

The most direct record-discrepancy example identified is Shanghai Otis Elevator Co., Ltd.'s Fengxian Powerlong Plaza matter, penalized in 2022. Electronic maintenance entries did not match video and actual work. The stated administrative classification was failure to maintain in accordance with safety technical specifications; the reported fine was RMB 10,000.

A falsely completed rescue work order must not automatically be called falsified maintenance. Short visits or frequent breakdowns alone cannot establish falsified records. Published inspection indicators are covered in [the inspection note](shanghai-inspection-rates.en.md); rescue-work-order issues require separate investigation. See also [cases outside Shanghai](china-maintenance.en.md).

## Legal and regulatory context recorded in the note

1. Article 45 of the Special Equipment Safety Law requires strict compliance with maintenance specifications and maintenance of safe performance.
2. Article 88 addresses non-compliant maintenance, including fines of RMB 10,000–100,000 and confiscation of illegal proceeds where applicable.
3. Elevator Maintenance Rules, TSG T5002—2017, address work items and records. Check the version applicable at the time.
4. Shanghai electronic and smart-elevator oversight may include lawful on-demand maintenance; absence of a visit every 15 days alone is not conclusive.

Sources: [Special Equipment Safety Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/tzsbj/zcfg/flfg/art/2019/art_39715c5a62f64e32aa522fcc2afd6298.html), [maintenance rules PDF](https://www.samr.gov.cn/cms_files/filemanager/samr/www/samrnew/tzsbj/zcfg/aqjsgf/202006/P020200622398888560704.pdf), [Shanghai elevator safety rules](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/xxzfgzwj/20230215/e09d5273bf0749818c6be962fd7c0734.html). This is a translation of previously assembled legal context, not a fresh statement of current law.

## Project-specific investigation method: Zizhu Huating

For elevators in use, first identify the actual maintenance entity, contract and applicable regime. Compare property-management fault logs, work orders, personnel identity and location, video, electronic photographs and smart-platform data. Where discrepancies exist, submit precise device identifiers and time windows to the Pudong market regulator for investigation.

Outsourced installation, frequent shutdowns or missing component-model information alone do not establish falsified maintenance or improper benefits. They are separate leads. This project-specific method is not evidence of bribery and is outside the historical bribery-case scope.

Before external reliance, check the original decision, entity, identifier, dates and legal basis.
