# Shanghai: Published Comprehensive Credit Rankings

> Unofficial English translation of the Chinese research note. [Chinese note](../docs/zh-CN/general-credit-ranking.md). Figures are carried over from the existing research, not independently rechecked during restructuring.

Publisher: Shanghai Administration for Market Regulation. Entities: 上海奥的斯电梯有限公司 (Shanghai Otis Elevator Co., Ltd.) and 奥的斯机电电梯有限公司上海分公司 (Shanghai Branch of Otis Electric Elevator Co., Ltd.). They are listed separately, not combined into a brand ranking.

| Maintenance entity | Quarter | 2025 rank / 12 entities | 2026 rank / 13 entities | Change |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Shanghai Otis | Q1 | 6 | 5 | Up 1 place |
| Shanghai Otis | Q2 | 4 | 3 | Up 1 place |
| Otis Electric, Shanghai Branch | Q1 | 7 | 12 | Down 5 places |
| Otis Electric, Shanghai Branch | Q2 | 10 | 11 | Down 1 place |

## Interpretation

These are published comprehensive credit rankings, not a ranking calculated solely from first-pass inspection rates. The category covers very large Shanghai maintenance providers with more than 5,000 elevators under maintenance. Both 2025 quarters had 12 participants; both 2026 quarters had 13. The participants and composition can change, so movement in rank cannot be read as an equivalent change in service quality.

The figures concern named Shanghai maintenance entities. They are not national brand rankings, manufacturing-quality rankings, new-elevator acceptance rates or operating failure rates. They do not establish bribery.
