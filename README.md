# Otis China: Public Records and Evidence Review

This repository organizes publicly available records concerning historical bribery matters involving Otis operations in China, distributors, and other potentially related parties.

It is intended to help reviewers trace each factual statement to its source, distinguish regulatory findings from allegations, and identify outstanding evidence gaps.

## Relationship to Prior SEC Enforcement

The SEC’s September 12, 2018 administrative order concerning United Technologies Corporation, Release No. **34-84087**, already addresses the Wuhan bank procurement matter and the Hangzhou and Shenzhen metro travel arrangements.

These matters are presented as previously documented conduct. Any additional material is identified separately; their inclusion does not imply a new violation or a separate enforcement action.

## Evidence and Translation

The planned evidence index will assign a unique identifier to each source. Case summaries will cite that identifier and, where available, the relevant page or paragraph.

Materials will be distinguished as:

- **Official records:** regulatory orders, judgments, and government documents directly obtained.
- **Secondary reporting:** reporting that describes or quotes records not independently obtained.
- **Unverified leads:** allegations or incomplete materials requiring further corroboration.

English translations will be labeled **“Unofficial English translation.”** Original Chinese names, case numbers, dates, and monetary amounts will be preserved. Translation uncertainties will be disclosed.

Planned storage:

- `evidence/originals/` — original materials lawfully obtained
- `evidence/translations/` — corresponding English translations
- `evidence/source-notes/` — source locations, access status, and passage references

## Scope and Limitations

This review relies on publicly accessible materials. It does not establish that all relevant proceedings or records have been identified.

An inability to locate a judgment does not establish that no proceeding occurred. An allegation, arrest, or prosecution does not establish a conviction. A regulatory finding is identified according to the nature and scope of the underlying proceeding.
