## Branch matching

Distinguish legal entity, branch, contracting party, payer, employer and incident location. A Shenyang branch can be involved in a Changchun incident; geography alone is insufficient.

Current [global roles](../background/global-leadership.en.md) and [reported 2025 branch changes](../background/china-regional-leadership.en.md) do not establish who held responsibility in 1996–2012. Inferred email patterns and screenshot transcriptions are unverified.

## Terminology

- “Sales supervisor,” “official” and “distributor” retain the source's level of specificity.
- Zhang, Wang and other anonymized names are not deanonymized by guessing.
- Chinese case numbers and penalty identifiers are retained unchanged.
- Group, brand and supplier relationships alone do not transfer knowledge, authorization or liability.
