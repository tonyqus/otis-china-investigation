# Jinju Xinyuan (金橘新苑)

[Directory](README.md)

## Project and complaint

Jinju Xinyuan is within **Dongming Road Subdistrict (东明路街道)**, according to the owner. A four-page, company-stamped letter dated **September 18, 2026** formally challenges the elevator replacement project's bidding and resident-consultation process.

The signatory is **Xiao Elevator (西奥电梯)**, specifically **杭州西奥电梯有限公司上海分公司** (Shanghai Branch of Hangzhou Xiao Elevator Co., Ltd.; descriptive English rendering). The letter describes the company as an invited bidder that submitted compliant bidding documents.

- **Addressed to:** the Jinju Xinyuan Owners' Committee and Property Service Center.
- **Copied to:** the Dongming Road Subdistrict Office and the Dongming Road Subdistrict Urban Construction Management Affairs Center, Pudong New Area.
- **Subject:** a formal challenge concerning alleged irregularities in bidding and resident consultation for the Jinju Xinyuan elevator replacement project.

## Five allegations in the letter

The following summarizes the complainant's allegations, not government findings:

| Allegation | What Xiao Elevator states | Letter pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preferential treatment through price changes | Otis's price announced at bid opening differed from the price later presented during resident consultation. Relevant parties allegedly allowed Otis to revise its quotation privately, while Xiao Elevator's quotation remained fixed throughout. | 1 |
| False claims about maintenance costs | Project-related personnel allegedly told residents that Xiao Elevator's maintenance charges and future upkeep costs were high, without consulting or obtaining confirmation from the company. The company disputes those statements. | 1–2 |
| Misleading price comparisons | Residents were allegedly told that Xiao Elevator's overall quotation was higher than Otis's. Xiao Elevator states its quotation was consistently lower than Otis's publicly announced bid-opening price. | 2 |
| Suppression of residents supporting Xiao Elevator | Citing reports from several residents who identified themselves, the letter alleges that resident representatives supporting Xiao Elevator were individually called in by neighborhood personnel and asked to stop speaking publicly. | 2–3 |
| Inadequate disclosure and guided voting | The consultation form allegedly listed only two brands without bid prices, technical proposals, equipment configurations, models or suitability comparisons. The letter also criticizes the proposed Otis equipment and alleges that personnel steered voting through brand recognition rather than full product information. These product criticisms are the competitor's assertions, not independently verified technical findings. | 3 |

The letter characterizes the project as an elevator replacement program supported by **government bond-funded subsidies**, raising concerns about public-fund use. Funding documents have not yet been reviewed.

## Requested action

Xiao Elevator asks the recipients and copied subdistrict authorities to:

- Suspend brand selection and further implementation.
- Conduct an open review of all bidders' prices, proposals, configurations and technical submissions; disclose original bid-opening quotations and meaningful comparisons.
- Investigate and explain the alleged private price changes and unequal evaluation treatment.
- Explain and remedy the alleged misinformation and interference with residents' views.
- Invalidate the disputed consultation results and repeat consultation with full disclosure and without steering or interference.
- Arrange subdistrict-level supervision and corrective action.
