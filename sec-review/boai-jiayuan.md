# Bo'ai Jiayuan (博爱家园)

[Directory](README.md)

## Project

According to the owner:

- **Location:** Zhoujiadu Subdistrict (周家渡街道), the same subdistrict as Zizhu Huating (紫竹华庭) and Changli Huayuan (昌里花园).
- **Elevators concerned:** 8 existing Shanghai Mitsubishi elevators, reported to have operated for approximately 25 years.
- **Replacement brand:** Otis, under a reported single-brand replacement arrangement.
- **Resident response:** residents in some buildings do not agree to replacement with Otis elevators.

Project dates, replacement/installation status, prices and contracting parties have not yet been supplied.

## Specific concern

The reported concern is the selection of Otis as the sole replacement brand despite opposition from residents in some buildings. The procurement process, alternatives offered, decision-making authority and building-by-building consultation or voting records remain to be established.

The shared subdistrict is a reported connection to the other Zhoujiadu projects. It does not establish that Bo'ai Jiayuan belongs to the same procurement project, involved the same personnel, or followed the same selection process.

## Evidence and source

Source: the owner's statement in the working conversation on November 16, 2026. No procurement notice, contract, equipment record, resident objection or voting record has yet been supplied for this project. The existing brand and service age are owner-reported; age alone does not establish equipment condition or the necessity of replacement.

The reported single-brand arrangement and resident objections are matters for verification, not established findings of unlawful procurement, bribery or an SEC violation.
