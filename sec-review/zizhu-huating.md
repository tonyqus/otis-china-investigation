## Specific concern

According to the owner, Zizhu Huating and Changli Huayuan form one procurement project organized at subdistrict level and restricted to the Otis brand. The owner identifies this as a single-brand directed-procurement concern. The owner identifies the shared subdistrict as **Zhoujiadu (周家渡街道)**; its specific procurement role, the procurement process, brand restriction and approval basis remain to be documented. A formal statutory single-source procurement procedure has not been established.

## Why the owner describes the procurement as single-brand directed procurement

The owner reports the following sequence for **Zizhu Huating**:

| Time | Reported event |
| --- | --- |
| May 2025, according to a later committee statement | The Owners' Committee later claimed that the Otis brand had already been selected in May 2025. According to the owner, the explanation was that Otis gave a presentation and the selection was approved by all Communist Party members. |
| Before the April 2026 voting announcement | Residents had not been informed which elevator brand would be proposed, according to the owner. |
| April 2026 | When a notice announcing a vote was issued, residents were told that Otis was the only brand available. No alternative brands were offered for selection, according to the owner. |
| When elevator installation began; exact date pending | The Owners' Committee issued a statement described as a rebuttal of rumors, stating that Otis had been selected in May 2025. The owner reports that the committee did not provide documentary evidence of a three-party price comparison or records addressing the owner's concern about possible cover bidding. |

The concern is that residents were presented with **a brand already chosen**, rather than a choice among competing brands, and that the earlier selection was explained only after installation began. The owner reports that the explanation relied on an Otis presentation and approval by Party members, without producing comparative procurement records.

### Reported authorization issue

The owner states that the development has approximately **50 households with Communist Party members**, fewer than two-thirds of resident households, and that **no owners' assembly was convened**. These are owner statements; the household count, total number of owners, property-area shares, attendance, ballot wording and voting results have not been verified.

Approval by Party members, as described in the owner's account, should not be represented as an owners' assembly resolution. Whether any legally sufficient written ballot or other owners' decision occurred, and which participation/approval rules applied, requires examination of the actual records and applicable rules. This note does not treat the owner's two-thirds comparison as an established legal test or conclude that a numerical threshold was violated.

The absence of documents provided to the owner does not itself establish that no comparison took place or that cover bidding occurred. “Single-brand directed procurement” describes the reported restriction and decision sequence; a formal statutory single-source procedure, bid-rigging, bribery and an unlawful procurement determination have not been established by the present record.
