# Scope and Limitations

The core review covers historical mainland China elevator-related bribery matters and public leads relevant to Otis or its distributors. It excludes overseas procurement and current residential-project allegations from its historical case count.

The package also translates background on organization, Shanghai inspection/credit indicators, maintenance/rescue compliance and civil litigation. These topics are not automatically evidence of FCPA, securities-law or bribery violations. SEC jurisdiction and any enforcement decision are for the competent authorities.

Latest substantive bribery review: November 13, 2026. English restructuring: November 14, 2026. Other source notes have their own research dates, including November 14 maintenance notes. Translation is not a fresh source verification date.

Public searches do not cover every judicial database, unpublished case file or administrative record. No located record does not mean no case. Official hosting of news does not turn reporting into a judgment. First-instance sentences reported by media are not represented as directly obtained final judgments.

Original external PDFs/pages are linked but not bundled unless explicitly listed as a stored artifact. No confidential internal records, witness declarations or original screenshot are claimed to be present.

This work does not send a report, contact a regulator or change repository visibility. A private repository link alone may not grant an external reviewer access; GitHub's existing access controls continue to apply. The repository can be downloaded as a source archive for authorized review.
