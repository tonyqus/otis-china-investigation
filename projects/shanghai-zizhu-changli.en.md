## Evidence status and records needed

All project particulars above are owner-supplied; amounts are calculated from the stated quantities and unit price. No supporting procurement notice, contract, equipment schedule or payment record has been supplied.

Obtain:

- Procurement/selection documents and the brand clause; decision records, justification/approval and quotations or reasons for their absence.
- Supplier-selection results and contracts identifying the subdistrict's, committees' and service providers' roles.
- Equipment schedules, pricing breakdowns, invoices and payment records confirming quantities, fee components, tax treatment, currency, period and payment status.
- Project addresses, acceptance/inspection records and documents supporting any specific concern.

The reported restriction is a matter to substantiate, not an established finding of unlawful procurement, bribery or an SEC violation. This project group is separate from the six historical matters.
