## Reported concern: subdistrict-level, single-brand directed procurement

The repository owner states that the two residential projects form **one procurement project organized at subdistrict (街道) level**, with **Otis as the only specified brand**. The owner identifies this as a problematic **single-brand directed procurement**, raising the question of whether brand restrictions excluded other elevator suppliers from competing.

This describes the owner's reported procurement arrangement and concern. “Single-brand directed procurement” is descriptive here; the statutory procurement method, including whether a formal single-source procedure was used, has not been established. The report does not by itself establish an unlawful procurement procedure, bribery or an SEC violation.

The reported subdistrict-level organization and the separately identified Owners’ Committees should both be documented: the responsible subdistrict, decision-maker, procuring/contracting parties, supplier-selection process and each committee's role remain to be confirmed from project records.

Obtain the procurement announcement/invitation, tender or selection documents, brand/specification clauses, any single-source justification and approval, competing quotations or reasons for their absence, decision/meeting records, supplier-selection result and signed contracts. These records should establish whether the Otis-only restriction existed, who imposed it, and what basis was given.
