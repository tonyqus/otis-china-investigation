# CN-006-shanghai-jin-yun: Shanghai Jin Yun Elevator Transaction

> English research summary, November 14, 2026. Unofficial English rendering; not a certified translation of a Chinese court record. [Chinese case extract](case-summary.zh-CN.md) · [Consolidated review](../../research/corruption-review.en.md).

## Summary and dates

News accounts describe an Otis sales supervisor receiving USD 20,000 after helping an intermediary prepare contracts with different prices.

Reported conduct: 1996. Investigation: 2000. Apprehension: August 18, 2014. News coverage: November–December 2014.

## Entities and participants

A government-hosted article is not a judicial decision. USD 160,000 and USD 20,000 must not be summed as independent losses. Corporate instructions or knowledge are not established.

## Supported or reported facts

The intermediary reportedly retained a USD 160,000 price difference. Reports say Jin Yun returned USD 20,000 during investigation, left China while proceedings were pending and was apprehended in Shanghai in 2014.

## Judicial or regulatory outcome

Investigation and apprehension reporting only. An indictment, final judgment, precise offense and sentence have not been obtained.

## Relationship to prior SEC enforcement and scope

This matter is not established as an additional finding in SEC Release 34-84087. It requires independent verification before reliance.

## Outstanding questions
