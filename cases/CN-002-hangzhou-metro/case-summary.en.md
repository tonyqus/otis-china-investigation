# CN-002-hangzhou-metro: Hangzhou Metro Line 1 Travel

> English research summary, November 14, 2026. Unofficial English rendering; not a certified translation of a Chinese court record. [Chinese case extract](case-summary.zh-CN.md) · [Consolidated review](../../research/corruption-review.en.md).

## Summary and dates

The SEC records an equipment-inspection trip that was in fact leisure travel for seven project-related officials.

Contract: 2008. Approval: 2010. Travel: 2011. SEC order: September 12, 2018.

## Entities and participants

One China matter in the 2018 order; not a new or separate SEC enforcement action. Contract value is not a bribery amount. No Chinese sentence is established.

## Supported or reported facts

A USD 27.6 million contract was obtained in 2008. An Otis China manager approved a 2011 trip to Italy and Greece. The purported subway-equipment inspections did not occur and the manager knew the purpose was leisure.

## Judicial or regulatory outcome

A finding in the UTC SEC administrative order. Chinese criminal or administrative follow-up has not been verified.

## Relationship to prior SEC enforcement and scope
