# CN-001-wuhan-bank: Wuhan State-Owned Bank Elevator Procurement

> English research summary, November 14, 2026. Unofficial English rendering; not a certified translation of a Chinese court record. [Chinese case extract](case-summary.zh-CN.md) · [Consolidated review](../../research/corruption-review.en.md).

## Summary and dates

The SEC describes an Otis China Wuhan sales supervisor arranging a distributor transaction to provide a kickback requested by a bank official.

Conduct: 2012. SEC order: September 12, 2018. Chinese conviction date: undisclosed.

## Entities and participants

Directly concerns Otis China sales operations. It does not identify the Chinese legal entity, person, bank or distributors sufficiently for independent matching. USD 98,000 is not necessarily the amount paid to the bank official.

## Supported or reported facts

Four elevator units were involved. The distributor transferred USD 98,000 to the supervisor, who paid a portion to the bank official and kept the remainder. Distributor justification and price spreads were not adequately challenged; an unauthorized distributor was inserted using a fake chop.

## Judicial or regulatory outcome

SEC paragraph 38 records that Chinese authorities convicted the supervisor in connection with the conduct before UTC learned of it. No Chinese judgment or specific sentence has been obtained.

## Relationship to prior SEC enforcement and scope

Already recorded in SEC Release 34-84087; this package supplies navigation and evidence boundaries, not a claim of newly discovered conduct.

## Outstanding questions

Chinese judgment and finality; exact statutory offense and sentence; identities; legal entities; contract and allocation of funds.
