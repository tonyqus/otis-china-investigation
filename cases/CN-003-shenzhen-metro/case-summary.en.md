# CN-003-shenzhen-metro: Shenzhen Metro Line 5 Travel

> English research summary, November 14, 2026. Unofficial English rendering; not a certified translation of a Chinese court record. [Chinese case extract](case-summary.zh-CN.md) · [Consolidated review](../../research/corruption-review.en.md).

## Summary and dates

The SEC records a US trip for seven officials whose claimed inspection activities did not take place.

Contract: 2008. Travel: 2011. SEC order: September 12, 2018.

## Entities and participants

The expenditure is a travel amount, not a Chinese court's assessed bribery amount. The contract value must not be treated as the bribe. Already covered in the 2018 UTC order.

## Supported or reported facts

The branch obtained a USD 3.3 million contract in 2008. In 2011 a major-project manager approved travel nominally for New York subway and Otis-facility inspections. The inspections did not occur; visits included New York and Washington, DC. Otis China spent USD 37,926.

## Judicial or regulatory outcome

SEC administrative findings; no Chinese criminal sentence or subsequent enforcement disposition has been obtained.

## Relationship to prior SEC enforcement and scope

Already recorded in SEC Release 34-84087; this package supplies navigation and evidence boundaries, not a claim of newly discovered conduct.

## Outstanding questions

Exact legal entities, travelers and approvals; Chinese administrative/criminal follow-up; travel and accounting records.
